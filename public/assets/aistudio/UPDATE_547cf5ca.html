Update to ASSEDGE

  1. Extract ASSEDGE.zip.
  2. Copy the files from its crm-side-panel folder into your existing installed crm-side-panel folder, replacing matching files. Keep the original folder location to preserve your extension ID and Google connection.
  3. Open chrome://extensions and click Reload on the existing CRM Side Panel extension. It will now be named ASSEDGE.
  4. Open ASSEDGE. It reconnects silently using your existing Google authorization. If Google needs sign-in again, enter a search and click Search or press Enter.

This update also removes the workspace heading and Connect Google button. Existing authorization is reused automatically. Asset shortcuts are limited to Folder, Opener, Memo and Link. Internal asset IDs are hidden; name and type appear without labels, with description, leader and team shown as plain text.

ASSEDGE now remembers the last search, filters, expanded records, number of visible results and scroll position during the current browser session. Closing and reopening the panel reuses cached records without Sheets requests. Refresh fetches current data. Closing the browser, reloading/updating/disabling the extension, or disconnecting clears the session cache. The cache is held in browser session memory, not written as a persistent CRM database.
